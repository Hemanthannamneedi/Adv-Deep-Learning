## Observations

GRU uses two gates and does not maintain a separate cell state. This makes its architecture simpler than LSTM.

LSTM uses a separate cell state and three gates to control information flow. GRU combines some of these operations into its update gate.

GRU often requires fewer parameters and may train faster. However, the accuracy and training time depend on the dataset, model configuration, and hardware.

## Conclusion

Both LSTM and GRU are recurrent neural network architectures designed to learn sequential patterns.

This experiment compares their parameter counts, training speed, and sentiment classification performance to understand their practical trade-offs.
