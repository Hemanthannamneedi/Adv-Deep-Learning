Artificial intelligence is changing the way people work, learn, and communicate. Machine learning systems identify patterns in data and use those patterns to make predictions. Deep learning uses neural networks to solve complex problems involving images, speech, and natural language.

Natural language processing helps computers understand and generate human language. Recurrent neural networks process sequential information by maintaining information from earlier steps. Long short-term memory networks are designed to learn long-term dependencies in sequences.

A language model learns patterns from text. At the character level, it receives a sequence of characters and predicts the next character. By repeating this process, the model can generate new text one character at a time.

Temperature controls the randomness of text generation. A low temperature produces more predictable output, while a high temperature produces more diverse output. The best temperature depends on the balance between creativity and coherence.

Learning deep learning requires experimentation, practice, and careful evaluation. Developers build models, test their performance, study the results, and improve their implementations. Python provides useful libraries for developing neural networks and natural language processing applications.
